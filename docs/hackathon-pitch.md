# SmartOT Command: Hackathon Pitch & Value Proposition

## 1. Problem Statement
Operating Theatres are a hospital's primary revenue engine and highest-cost operational asset (costing upwards of \$25 to \$60 per minute). Yet, surgeries routinely experience cascading delays caused by simple, preventable communication gaps:
- ❌ Missing consent or incomplete checklists in inpatient wards
- ❌ Missing or expired sterile instrument packs from CSSD
- ❌ Inpatient transport delays and uncoordinated room turnovers
- ❌ Expensive legacy Hospital Information Systems (HIS) that act as passive data silos without predictive workflow intelligence.

---

## 2. Our Solution: SmartOT Command
SmartOT Command is an affordable operational intelligence platform that connects Admissions, Wards, CSSD, and Operating Theatres into a correlated stream of workflow events.

### Value Proposition:
1. **CONNECT**: Seamlessly correlates multi-department telemetry.
2. **TRACK**: 6-point pre-op readiness and QR-based instrument validation.
3. **UNDERSTAND**: Explainable root-cause attribution for every schedule overrun.
4. **PREDICT**: Quantifies cascading downstream delay risk on later surgeries.
5. **RECOMMEND**: Real-time AI Operations Consultant and Next-Best-Action priority queue.

---

## 3. Measurable ROI & Impact
- 📈 **+4.8% to +6.5% OT Utilization Gain** through optimized turnover and ward transport.
- ⏱️ **~105 Minutes of Daily Surgical Capacity Recovered** across a 4-theatre suite.
- 🏥 **+6 Additional Elective Procedures Booked Weekly** without hiring additional staff or building new rooms.
- 🛡️ **Zero Data Loss** in low-connectivity hospitals via offline IndexedDB sync.
